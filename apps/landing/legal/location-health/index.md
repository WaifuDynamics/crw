# Location & Health Data Notice

_CRW+ · Last updated: 17 September 2026 · https://crw-plus.com/legal/location-health/_

## 1. Precise location

CRW+ uses precise location to track active workouts, calculate route, distance, pace, and related statistics. CRW+ does not intend to continuously track your location when no workout is active.

## 2. Route storage

Workout routes may be stored on CRW+ servers to provide workout history and social features. Where the local-only option is available and enabled, route data is kept on the device instead of being uploaded to CRW+ servers.

## 3. Visibility

Users can use available privacy controls to hide workouts or limit visibility, including friends-only visibility where supported. Before sharing a route publicly, consider whether it may reveal your home, workplace, school, or other sensitive location.

## 4. Apple Health / HealthKit and Health Connect

Integration is optional. CRW+ may request access to workouts, heart rate, steps, calories, distance, and other fitness-related data needed for a user-facing CRW+ feature. CRW+ does not intentionally request sleep or menstrual-cycle data.

## 5. No advertising use

CRW+ does not sell health or fitness integration data and does not use such data for targeted advertising or marketing profiles.

## 6. User control

You can decline or revoke location and health permissions through your device settings. Some CRW+ features will not work correctly without the permissions they require.

## 7. Accuracy

GPS, wearable, phone sensor, HealthKit, and Health Connect data may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate. CRW+ should not be used as a medical measurement device.
