# Community Guidelines

_CRW+ · Last updated: 17 September 2026 · https://crw-plus.com/legal/guidelines/_

CRW+ is designed to be a safe, inclusive fitness community. These rules apply to profiles, photos, workouts, clubs, events, usernames, and any other user-generated content.

## 1. Be respectful

Do not harass, bully, threaten, stalk, shame, or target another person. Do not encourage others to attack or intimidate a user.

## 2. No hate or dehumanizing content

Do not attack people based on protected characteristics or promote hatred, dehumanization, or violence against protected groups.

## 3. No sexual or exploitative content

Do not upload pornography, explicit sexual content, sexual exploitation, or sexual content involving minors. Profile photos may be checked by basic anti-NSFW systems and may be removed after automated or human review.

## 4. No threats or dangerous conduct

Do not post credible threats, encourage violence, organize harmful conduct, or use CRW+ to endanger another person.

## 5. No fraud or impersonation

Do not impersonate people or organizations, operate scams, misrepresent events, or attempt to obtain money, credentials, or personal information through deception.

## 6. No spam or manipulation

Do not send repetitive unwanted content, manipulate rankings, create fake engagement, mass-report users maliciously, or use automated abuse.

## 7. Protect privacy

Do not publish another person's private information, precise location, route, contact details, or other sensitive data without permission.

## 8. Clubs and events

Club and event descriptions must be accurate and lawful. Organizers are responsible for their real-world event operations, participant communication, and any separate fees collected outside CRW+.

## 9. Reporting

If you see content that violates these Guidelines, use the in-app reporting tools. CRW+ may review reports, remove content, restrict access, suspend accounts, permanently remove accounts, or take other proportionate action.

## 10. Appeals and mistakes

If you believe moderation action was taken in error, contact support@crw-plus.com with your @username and a short explanation. CRW+ may review the decision but does not guarantee reinstatement.
