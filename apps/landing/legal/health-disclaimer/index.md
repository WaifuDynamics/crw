_CRW+ · Last updated: 17 September 2026 · https://crw-plus.com/legal/health-disclaimer/_

## 1. General fitness only

CRW+ provides workout tracking, fitness statistics, community features, and general lifestyle content such as healthy-food or recipe inspiration. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or individualized clinical recommendations.

## 2. Consult a professional when appropriate

If you have a medical condition, symptoms, an injury, are pregnant, take medication, or are unsure whether exercise is appropriate for you, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional before starting or changing an exercise routine.

## 3. Measurements are estimates

Calories, heart rate, distance, pace, speed, steps, routes, and other values may be estimates and may vary because of device sensors, wearables, GPS signal, permissions, environmental conditions, and third-party integrations.

## 4. Food and recipe content

Food suggestions and recipes are general informational content and are not nutritional or medical treatment plans. Users are responsible for checking ingredients, allergies, dietary restrictions, and suitability.

## 5. Exercise risk

Physical activity involves risk. Users are responsible for choosing activities appropriate to their circumstances and environment and for following local safety rules.
