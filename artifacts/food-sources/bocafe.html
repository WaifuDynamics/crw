Member benefits & access

Member discounts — Premium members: 10% off eligible BôCafé purchases. VIP Wellness members: 15% off eligible BôCafé purchases. Discounts apply only to eligible items and the registered member's own purchase, and cannot be combined with other promotions unless approved.

Open to everyone — you do not need to be a Gymbo member to visit BôCafé; local residents, professionals and visitors are welcome. Allergen information: ingredients, portion standards and allergen information are managed through approved recipes and operating procedures. Speak with the team before ordering if you have an allergy or dietary concern.

Good to know

How BôCafé works.

Open to everyone

You do not need a Gymbo membership to eat or drink at BôCafé. Walk in, sit down, stay a while.

Member discounts

Premium members receive 10% off and VIP Wellness members 15%, applied at the counter on presentation of your membership.

Made for training

Recovery smoothies, protein options, bowls, wraps and light meals designed to sit well before or after a session.

Dietary information

Allergen and dietary details are available for every item — ask the team and we will tell you exactly what is in it.
